LinkedIn sourcing · account safety first

Choose manual import or enable public guest search after reviewing two separate warnings.

⚠
Account-lock warning: Automated scraping or browser automation may violate LinkedIn's terms and could trigger restrictions or account shutdown. No tool, low request rate, or warning checkbox can guarantee that an account will remain safe. Read LinkedIn's official policy ↗. Never share your password, cookies, session token or browser profile with CareerPilot.
Warning 1 of 2 · Public LinkedIn job search

CareerPilot can request public, unauthenticated LinkedIn guest job pages like the reference AI Job Search project. LinkedIn's terms may prohibit automated access. Even public pages can return rate limits or lead to account restrictions; no warning can guarantee account safety. CareerPilot will not use your login, cookies, browser session, CAPTCHA bypass, proxy rotation or automatic retries after access denial.

Warning 2 of 2 · Explicit opt-in

Search is limited to 5 searches per day, at least 60 seconds apart, and 10 results per search. Job-detail fetches are limited. If LinkedIn denies or rate-limits access, CareerPilot automatically pauses the feature and will not retry. Results may be incomplete or stale; verify listings yourself.
