CAREERPILOT GUIDE

How to use your job search workspace

Use Job Discovery to open live job-board searches. These are search links and curated search lanes, not a live feed or confirmed job vacancies.

Use Resume Studio to paste the actual job description and create a first-pass tailored text resume. The draft is deliberately conservative and should be checked against the source resume before use.

Save leads to track them. The current tracker and profile are saved in the browser on this device, not synced across devices or protected by a personal account.

For work visas and permanent residence, check the exact employer, current official immigration criteria, occupation rules, salary thresholds and permit requirements. A job offer alone does not guarantee sponsorship or PR.
